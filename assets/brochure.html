BYD DOLPHIN SURF

Ocean Series · Product overview

The BYD DOLPHIN SURF is the most individual and dynamic member of the Ocean Series. It combines Ocean Aesthetics design, the ultra-safe Blade Battery, and e-Platform 3.0 in a compact pure-electric city car.

Highlights

  • Blade Battery — ultra-safe cell-to-body architecture
  • 30 minutes — typical 30% to 80% DC fast charge
  • e-Platform 3.0 — intelligence, efficiency, safety, aesthetics
  • Comprehensive airbag protection, including curtain airbags
