Hybrid grunt

BYD SHARK 6 comes equipped with a Hybrid Special 1.5 litre turbo high-power engine that, in conjunction with the BYD Blade Battery, boasts a massive 321 kW of power and groundbreaking 650 N·m of torque maximum output — with 2,500 kg braked towing capacity for any lifestyle.

DMO. Off-Road Hybrid System

BYD SHARK 6 features an AWD plug-in hybrid architecture built from the ground up to maximise comfort and efficiency — the world’s first longitudinal EHS Super Hybrid architecture.

Blade Battery

A 29.58 kWh BYD Blade Battery supports pure-electric driving with V2L functionality, turning the SHARK 6 into a mobile power source.

Built for every terrain

Double wishbone front and rear suspension with Mud, Sand and Snow terrain modes, plus Hill Hold Control, Vehicle Dynamic Control, Hill Descent Control and Auto-Hold for off-road tasks.

Off-road chassis

A high-integrated, high-performance off-road chassis with high-strength steel underbody protection.

Improved vehicle stability

Long-travel double-wishbone suspension stabilises SHARK 6 around corners, with Intelligent Power Brake and Comfort Stop for smoother driving.

Cell to Chassis

Integrated battery architecture increases rigidity by 22%, delivering ultra-safe structure and increased load capacity.

Premium interior

Family practicality meets an intelligent driver cockpit — with a 15.6-inch intelligent rotating touchscreen, 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster, W-HUD, and a 12-speaker Dynaudio sound system.

Connected comfort

Portable charging cable, V2L, wireless phone charger, USB-C and USB-A front and rear, Bluetooth phone connectivity, DAB digital radio, and voice assistant.

Always up to date

Advanced driver assist systems with OTA updates keep the SHARK 6 ready for the road ahead.

Safe and secure

Child presence detection, 7 SRS airbags — front, side (front & rear), and side curtain (front & rear) — ISOFIX child restraint anchorage points on the outer rear seats, plus front and rear cross-traffic alert with braking.

Re-defining utility

Dedicated rear drivetrain assembly designed specifically for utility vehicles, with intelligent turbo power and longitudinal EHS for work, weekend, and everything in between.
