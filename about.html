About BYD

Founded in 1994, BYD is a high-tech company devoted to leveraging technological innovations for a better life.

After more than 30 years of growth, BYD works across electronics, automobiles, renewable energy, and rail transit. With a focus on energy acquisition, storage, and application, it offers comprehensive zero-emission new energy solutions.
