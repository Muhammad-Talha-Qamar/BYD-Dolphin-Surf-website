BYD Super DM Technology

Super DM (Dual Mode) is BYD’s intelligent plug-in hybrid system: electricity first, with the engine ready when you need it.

You can identify BYD plug-in hybrids by the DM in the name. In SEALION 6 DM-i, the “i” stands for intelligent, energy-efficient driving.

How it works

  • A high-power electric motor and large-capacity Blade Battery are the primary power sources.
  • The Xiaoyun hybrid engine provides support when extra range or performance is needed.
  • The Electric Hybrid System (EHS) integrates dual motors, dual controllers, and oil-cooling for density and efficiency.

Economical

Energy-efficient hybrid driving with low fuel consumption on long trips.

Fast

Responsive electric torque, with AWD 0–100 km/h in 5.9 s on SEALION 6 DM-i.

Quiet & smooth

Electric-first running keeps journeys calm, with seamless hybrid assistance.
