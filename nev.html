What is a NEV?

NEV stands for New Energy Vehicle — a broader term than EV, covering the main alternatives to conventional petrol and diesel cars.

The main types

  • BEV (Battery Electric Vehicle) — driven only by an electric motor and battery, such as the DOLPHIN SURF and SEALION 6.
  • PHEV (Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicle) — a large battery you can plug in, plus an engine for longer journeys, such as the SEALION 6 DM-i.
  • HEV (Hybrid Electric Vehicle) — petrol-electric assistance without plugging in.

BYD is one of the world’s largest NEV manufacturers. In Egypt the current passenger lineup includes compact BEV, family BEV SUV, and Super DM plug-in hybrid SUV.
