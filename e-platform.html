e-Platform 3.0

A dedicated architecture for 100% electric vehicles, built around intelligence, efficiency, safety, and aesthetics.

e-Platform 3.0 is designed to improve NEV safety, low-temperature driving range, and intelligent driving, so BYD can build more efficient and safer electric cars.

Safety

Integrating the Blade Battery into the body doubles vehicle rigidity. The battery is both an energy store and a structural part.

High efficiency

The world’s first mass-produced 8-in-1 electric powertrain takes overall system efficiency up to 89%.

Intelligence

An integrated domain-controlled architecture supports continuous over-the-air upgrades of the whole vehicle.

Aesthetics

Shorter overhangs and a longer wheelbase free up cabin space and aerodynamic design.

See it on the DOLPHIN SURF and SEALION 6.
