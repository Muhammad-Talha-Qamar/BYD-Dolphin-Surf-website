Ocean-inspired design

The BYD SEALION 6 applies captivating design philosophy to the SUV market by combining the oceanic aesthetics of BYD design with futuristic touches, resulting in a striking car for eco-conscious families.

Projecting a greater light

The double U-shaped headlights incorporate staggered light clusters that cast a powerful beam, ensuring clearer visibility. The rear exudes sophistication with a continuous taillight designed in a refined water-drop matrix pattern.

A spacious family cabin

A light, airy interior with a rotatable centre screen, panoramic roof, and generous rear seating makes the SEALION 6 a comfortable electric SUV for everyday family life.

Always-in-touch rotatable screen

A large rotatable touchscreen acts as the central hub for connectivity and infotainment, with intelligent voice control and real-time information at a glance.

Light and airy panoramic roof

The expansive panoramic sunroof fills the cabin with natural light, helping passengers feel more connected to the world outside.

Innovation

BYD has been a pioneering name in the battery industry for more than 29 years. The Blade Battery in the SEALION 6 has passed a series of extreme tests, making it one of the world’s safest batteries.

The world’s first mass-produced 8-in-1 electric powertrain

BYD incorporates the VCU, BMS, MCU, PDU, DC-DC controller, on-board charger, drive motor, and transmission into one system, maximising space utilisation and energy efficiency.

High-efficiency heat pump as standard

The advanced heat pump operates across a broad temperature range, using residual heat to reduce energy loss and improve low-temperature driving range.

BYD Blade Battery

Featured in the BYD SEALION 6, the Blade Battery brings ultra-safe LFP chemistry and structural strength exclusive to BYD’s electric platform.
