Data Privacy

How personal data from this website is handled when you enquire about a BYD model in Egypt.

Who receives your data

If you submit the dealer enquiry form, the message is created in your email application and addressed to BYD Egypt. Nothing is uploaded to a server from this static website.

What we ask for

  • Name and phone number, so a store can call you back
  • The model you are interested in
  • Any extra notes you choose to write

Your rights

You can ask how your enquiry was handled by contacting bydautoservice.mea@byd.com or calling 08000060640.
