BYD Blade Battery

Recognised as one of the world’s safest EV batteries, the Blade Battery is designed to maximise strength, range, and life cycle.

BYD has been a pioneering name in the battery industry for more than 29 years. Lithium iron phosphate chemistry, a flat cell layout, and pack-level structure give the Blade Battery its distinctive safety and durability.

Enhanced safety

LFP chemistry has slow heat generation and does not release oxygen. The Blade Battery has passed the nail penetration test without emitting fire or smoke.

Optimised strength

Cells arranged as structural beams, with honeycomb-like pack design, help the battery withstand force and add rigidity to the vehicle.

Longer range

Space utilisation is increased by over 50% versus traditional packs, supporting higher energy density and longer driving range.

Longer lifecycle

The Blade Battery is engineered for a long service life, supporting up to 1,000,000 km of total driving.

BYD DOLPHIN SURF and BYD SEALION 6 both use Blade Battery technology.
